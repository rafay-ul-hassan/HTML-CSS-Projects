Tribute to Imran Khan

Supporters and admirers widely view Imran Khan as a symbol of courage, resilience, and an unwavering fight for justice. From lifting the 1992 Cricket World Cup to building Pakistan's first specialized cancer hospital, his life reflects a continuous dedication to public service and national dignity. Despite facing intense political trials and imprisonment in later years, his followers honor him as a leader who challenged traditional political corruption and inspired a generation to demand accountability and self-reliance.
